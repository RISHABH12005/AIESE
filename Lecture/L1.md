## Software Requirements – Surveillance Drone

### Functional Requirements
1. The system shall provide remote control of the surveillance drone.
2. The system shall support take-off, landing, hovering, and movement.
3. The system shall capture and transmit live video.
4. The system shall detect people, vehicles, and other predefined objects.
5. The system shall generate alerts for detected suspicious activities.
6. The system shall track the drone using GPS.
7. The system shall record surveillance videos and detected events.
8. The system shall detect and avoid obstacles.
9. The system shall monitor the drone's battery level.
10. The system shall provide automatic return-to-home functionality.
11. The system shall authenticate authorized users.
12. The system shall maintain activity and flight logs.

### Non-Functional Requirements
1. The system shall provide near real-time video transmission.
2. The system shall respond quickly to control commands.
3. The system shall operate reliably under normal environmental conditions.
4. The system shall protect communication and stored data from unauthorized access.
5. The system shall provide an easy-to-use control interface.
6. The system shall be maintainable and modular.
7. The system shall support future integration of additional sensors and AI models.
8. The system shall provide safe operation during communication or system failures.
9. The system shall operate within specified battery, weather, and communication limits.
10. The system shall comply with applicable drone safety and privacy regulations.
